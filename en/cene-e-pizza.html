Evenings at Al Bistrò

On Friday and Saturday evenings the kitchen and the pizzeria work side by side: tartare and sharing boards, paccheri with seafood, grilled spring chicken, sliced steak, burger, mixed fried seafood, or one of our 28 pizzas. And there is always a dish of the day.

The pizza: a classic slow-leavened dough made with fine flours: light and fragrant. It rises naturally for 24 hours and is baked in a stone oven.
