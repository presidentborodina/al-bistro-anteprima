Privacy and cookies

Provisional text, to be checked before the website goes live.

Who processes your data

Al Bistrò, Via delle Ande 16, 20151 Milan, VAT no. IT09975970964. For any request about your data: info@albistro.it.

What data we collect

Only what you write to us yourself: name, phone number, email address and the content of your request, when you fill in a form, message us on WhatsApp or send us an email.

Why we use it

To reply to your request for information, a quote or a booking. If you give your consent, also to let you know about evenings and events: you can withdraw it at any time by writing to info@albistro.it.

How the forms work

The forms on the website open WhatsApp with the message already written: your data is only sent when you send it from WhatsApp, which also processes it under its own rules.

Cookies

The website does not use profiling cookies or analytics tools. Pages with a map load Google Maps, which may use its own cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to info@albistro.it. You can also contact the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
