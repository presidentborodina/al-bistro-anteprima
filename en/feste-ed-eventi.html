Your party at Al Bistrò

The party room is a dehors of its own, separate from the restaurant: reserved just for you, heated and double-glazed. In summer the windows can be opened onto the greenery, or you can stay out in the garden.

  • SpacesParty room 50 people, restaurant dining room 40, garden 40
  • Just for youThe party room is separate from the restaurant; for larger parties the whole venue can be reserved
  • MenuAgreed for each party
  • MusicYour own music until midnight. On request, a live musician and karaoke
  • CakeYou can bring your own, with the allergen list: cake-cutting service € 2 per person
  • AccessFor wheelchair users, with help from the staff

Parties at Al Bistrò

Three spaces, all year round

Party room

Up to 50 people seated. A heated, glazed dehors, independent from the restaurant: you can sing and dance until midnight.

Restaurant dining room

Up to 40 people, with the open pizza counter. For a company dinner or a family lunch.

Garden

Up to 40 people, among the greenery. For a standing aperitif or a summer party.

How to get here

On foot

10 minutes from the Uruguay stop on metro line M1, 15 from Lampugnano.

By car

5 minutes from Bonola, 7 from Trenno, 8 from QT8, 9 from San Siro. Parking is available on Viale De Gasperi and Via delle Ande.

Frequently asked questions

How many people does the party room hold?

There are 50 seats in the dehors, 40 in the restaurant dining room and 40 in the garden. For larger parties the whole venue can be reserved.

How much does a party cost per person?

Everything is agreed in advance, based on the number of people and your personalised menu; the price is calculated per person.

Can I bring a cake?

Yes, with the bakery's allergen list. The cake-cutting service costs € 2 per person.

Until what time can we party?

Until midnight.

There are children and people with intolerances.

The menu is tailored to your needs and requests (gluten-free, lactose-free, vegetarian).

How far in advance should I book?

The sooner you write, the more dates you will find free.

Can I see the room first?

Of course: drop in and we will give you all the information.
