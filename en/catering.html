Frequently asked questions

How much does catering cost per person?

Every catering job is tailor-made: the price depends on the menu, the number of people and the service. Send us the date, the number of guests and the type of event: we will reply with a proposal and a price.

How many people do you cater for?

From a small aperitif at the office to a reception: there is no minimum, we tailor the proposal to your numbers.

Who brings the plates, glasses and tablecloths?

We do, as you prefer: professional eco-friendly disposables with paper napkins, or china plates, glass stemware, steel cutlery and cotton table linen.

We have vegetarians and a coeliac guest: how do you handle that?

Every proposal can include vegetarian, gluten-free and lactose-free dishes. Just mention it in the form.

How far in advance should I ask?

The sooner you write, the better. For a date coming up soon, call us directly on +39 391 176 1595.

Can we hold the event at your place?

Yes: the party room is glazed and heated, separate from the restaurant, and seats up to 50 people. In summer there is the garden too.
